RiskScope — Asset-Aware Vulnerability Exposure & Risk Prioritization Workstation

You are implementing RiskScope, a local-first vulnerability exposure, asset-risk analysis, and remediation prioritization workstation for security analysts, vulnerability-management teams, infrastructure/security engineers, and incident responders.

RiskScope must NOT be a simple CVE database, vulnerability scanner, patch tracker, or generic dashboard.

Its core purpose is to answer:

Which vulnerabilities actually matter in this environment, why do they matter, what assets are affected, how exposed are those assets, and what should be remediated first?

RiskScope should correlate:

Assets
   ↓
Software
   ↓
Versions
   ↓
Vulnerabilities
   ↓
Exploitability
   ↓
Network Exposure
   ↓
Asset Criticality
   ↓
Attack Paths
   ↓
Risk Prioritization
   ↓
Remediation
   ↓
Verification

The product must be evidence-driven.

Every important risk assessment must be explainable through its underlying data.

Do not invent unexplained risk scores.

2. Core Product Philosophy

RiskScope must follow these principles.

2.1 Risk is contextual

Do not assume:

CVSS 9.8 = highest priority

Instead evaluate:

CVSS
+
Exploitability
+
Known Exploitation
+
Internet Exposure
+
Asset Criticality
+
Business Context
+
Privilege Impact
+
Affected Service
+
Attack-Path Position

The system must explain why a vulnerability receives a particular priority.

2.2 Evidence first

Every risk assessment must be traceable to evidence.

For example:

Why?

⸻

2.3 Separate facts from interpretation

RiskScope must distinguish:

Observations

Facts imported or discovered from data.

Automated assessment

Rules and calculations performed by RiskScope.

Analyst assessment

Human interpretation and final decision.

For example:

Observation:
WEB-01 is internet-facing.
Automated assessment:
CVE-XXXX has high exposure.
Analyst conclusion:
Prioritize remediation within 7 days because WEB-01 hosts a production authentication service.
